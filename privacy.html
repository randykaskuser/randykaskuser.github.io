Privacy policy

proxmox-backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

  • Data accessed: files in the owner's own Google Drive, through the Google Drive API, to upload and update backup copies.
  • Use: the data is used only to store and restore the owner's backups.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored only on the owner's private server.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
